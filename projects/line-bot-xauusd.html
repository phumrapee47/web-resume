Project Overview & Motivation

Retail traders frequently suffer from "black box" signals where automated bots provide buy/sell directions without transparent reasoning or risk context. The LINE Bot XAUUSD system solves this problem by pairing a machine learning price action classifier with **Google Gemini LLM** to produce human-interpretable technical explanations, risk metrics, and key price levels for every alert.

📈

Machine Learning Signal Detection

Real-time algorithmic scanning evaluating multi-timeframe price structure, RSI divergence, MACD crossovers, and ATR volatility breakout levels.

🧠

Gemini AI Reasoning Engine

Generates structured analysis justifying why the setup is favorable, highlighting potential risk factors, invalidation prices, and optimal risk-reward targets.

💬

Automated LINE Push Notifications

Dispatches richly formatted messages to LINE subscribers containing entry zones, stop losses, take profits, and AI narrative summaries.

⚡

24/5 Live Market Monitoring

Continuous background polling with smart anti-duplicate filters preventing notification spam during choppy sideway market conditions.
