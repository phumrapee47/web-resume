Eat@Sci: Cafeteria Mobile Application

A cross-platform mobile solution developed for the Faculty of Science cafeteria at KMITL, featuring automated institutional student verification, live QR order tracking, and vendor dashboard management.

Client / Target Faculty of Science, KMITL
Framework Flutter & Dart
Database & Auth Supabase (PostgreSQL RLS)

Project Overview & Problem Solved

During peak lunchtime hours at the Faculty of Science cafeteria, overcrowding and long queue times frequently caused student schedule delays. **Eat@Sci** modernizes the dining experience by allowing students to pre-order food, upload payment slips, and pick up meals seamlessly using unique QR code verification.

🎓

Auto Institutional Registration

Automatically parses student ID numbers, faculty branch, and academic year level directly from KMITL email addresses during authentication.

📱

QR Verification & Slip Uploads

Generates dynamic pickup QR codes upon order submission with integrated promptpay slip uploads for fast vendor verification.

🏪

Merchant & Kitchen Mode

Dedicated merchant view for vendor stalls to manage menu offerings, set sold-out flags, and update cooking progress in real-time.

🔒

Supabase Row-Level Security

Strict database isolation policies ensure students only access their own order histories and personal records.

Technical Architecture & Stack

Application Workflow

Real-Time Mobile Flow
1
Student Auth & Profile Generation

Student logs in with KMITL Google OAuth → Regex parser extracts year/faculty → Profile synchronized in Supabase.

2
Menu Browsing & Pre-Ordering

Student selects vendor stall, chooses dishes, specifies food customizations, and checks out with payment slip attachment.

3
Vendor Kitchen Real-Time Processing

Vendor receives push alert, verifies payment slip, prepares food, and marks the order as "Ready for Pickup".

4
QR Code Pickup & Completion

Student presents dynamic QR code at the stall counter → Vendor scans to confirm handover and close ticket.
